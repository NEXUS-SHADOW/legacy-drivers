Lexmark 5000 Protocol

Command types

There are two types of commands in the 5000's repertoire:

  • Escape asterisk commands commencing with the two-byte sequence `0x1b 0x2a' (Escape Asterisk).  These sub-divide into four classes:
    • Escape m
      The third byte is `0x6d'; the ASCII 'm'.
    • Escape 7
      The third byte is `0x7'; the ASCII BEL.
    • Escape 3
      The third byte is '0x3'; the ASCII ETX or Ctrl-C.
      This is the vertical paper movement command, followed by a two-byte relative move.
    • Escape 4
      The third byte is '0x4'; the ASCII EOT or Ctrl-D.
      This is the swipe print command, followed by a four-byte total command length (including header), and the remainder of the swipe command.
  • A5 commands commencing with the byte `0xa5'.
    The second & third bytes are the MSB and LSB of the length of the remainder of the command.

Detailed discussion

  • Printheads & nozzle configuration

    A discussion of the configuration of the nozzles in the printheads of the cartridges.  Essential for an understanding of the swipe command.

  • Initialisation, maintenance & paper movement commands

    Very little is known about the specific functions of individual initialisation commands.  In general, a collection of these appears at the beginning of each page.

    There are only two known paper movement commands, so this bit is easy.

  • The swipe command

    The heart of the driver is the swipe command, which does almost all of the work.
